# ORIGO — Foundation

## 1. Sovereignty
The user controls their data and computing environment.

## 2. Workspace
Launch directly into the workspace. Do not require a landing screen.

## 3. Arrangement
The user manually arranges panels and tools. Do not silently rearrange
the desktop workspace.

## 4. Restoration
Restore the last saved workspace layout. Do not automatically restart
previously active commands, operations, or agents.

## 5. Portability
Design for desktop, Chromebook, and mobile. Preserve the user's intent
when a smaller screen requires a different presentation.

## 6. Transparency
Make background activity visible. Distinguish observation from execution.

## 7. Separation of concerns
- Environment: workspace and presentation.
- Projects: files and project configuration.
- CODE: software construction.
- SIGNAL: investigation and integration.
- SIGNALWEAVER: shared continuity.
- Execution: commands, runtimes, and permissions.
- Observer: cross-cutting observation controls.

These are conceptual boundaries, not yet a finalized implementation.

## 8. Uncertainty
Distinguish proposed ideas from confirmed decisions and completed work.
Do not silently convert assumptions into facts.
